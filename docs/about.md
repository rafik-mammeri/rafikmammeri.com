# About

I came to AI through mathematics. My PhD taught me to approach problems that have no ready-made answer yet, starting from the theory rather than treating a model as a black box.
{ .rm-lede }

I spent six years at BNP Paribas: first modelling credit risk under banking regulation, where every model has to be documented and defended, then at the Datalab, taking machine-learning projects to production for different business lines. That is where I learned to design a system on the assumption that the model will one day be wrong.

Since 2025, I have been a Lead AI Engineer at Boulanger, tech lead of a team of AI engineers that ships agents to production for customers and employees. I live in Lille, France.
