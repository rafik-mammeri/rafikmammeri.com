# About

I started in mathematics. I did a PhD at the University of Lille and taught there. What stayed with me is a way of working: take a problem nobody has solved yet, and stay with it until it makes sense.
{ .rm-lede }

In 2018 I moved to Bordeaux to join BNP Paribas. I built credit-risk models in a world where every decision has to be explained to regulators. It taught me rigour, and a habit I still have: assume the model will be wrong one day, and plan for it.

In 2021 I joined the bank's data and AI lab in Paris. The work changed from building models to getting them used: four projects taken all the way to production, each handed over to the teams who would run it.

When generative AI arrived, I wanted to build with it rather than watch. I spent six months as an independent engineer, taking a product on my own from the first line of code to production.

In 2025 I joined Boulanger to build its customer assistant. One project led to the next: a second-generation shopping assistant, a voice bot for the customer-service line, tools for employees. Along the way I became the tech lead of the team that builds them: I set the technical direction and make sure every engineer can ship with confidence.

I live in Lille.
